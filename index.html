This webpage is to celebrate hispanic heritage month through showcasing Mon Laferte's talents and achievements.

About Mon Laferte

Mon Laferte is a Chilean-Mexican singer and songwriter. She has won multiple awards for her music, including Latin Grammy Awards. Her music style includes pop, rock, and bolero, and she is known for her powerful voice and emotional performances.

Here are some of her most popular songs:

  • 1. Tu Falta De Querer
  • 2. Mi Buen Amor
  • 3. Amarrame
  • 4. Si Tu Me Quieres
  • 5. Melancolia
